Privacy Policy Lingua Atlas ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. Information We Collect Account information: name and email address, used to create and manage your account. Profile information: gender and country, used to personalize your experience. These are optional and used for app functionality only. Learning progress: quiz results, XP, streaks, and achievements, used to track your progress and personalize recommendations. Voice/audio data: when you use the Speak It pronunciation feature, your speech is processed to score pronunciation accuracy. Device and advertising identifiers: used by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) to show and measure ads. Crash and diagnostic data: used to identify and fix technical issues. How We Use Information We use the information above to operate and improve the app, personalize your learning experience, provide customer support, and serve advertising that supports the app being free to use. Third-Party Services We use Google AdMob to display advertising. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, in accordance with your consent choices, which you can review anytime from the app's Privacy settings. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with service providers (such as our hosting and advertising partners) as needed to operate the app. Your Choices You can review and adjust your advertising consent choices at any time in the app's Privacy settings.
