Lingua Atlas Lingua Atlas is a mobile app that helps people learn new languages in a way that's structured, engaging, and genuinely effective. It currently supports 21 languages, including Spanish, French, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese, making it accessible to learners from a wide range of backgrounds and goals — whether they're studying for travel, connecting with family, preparing for school, or simply exploring a language out of curiosity. The app is built around the idea that language learning works best when it combines structured study with active practice. On the study side, learners can work through vocabulary word banks organized by difficulty level, master the alphabet and numbers from scratch, review core grammar rules, and explore cultural context and common proverbs that textbooks often skip. Topic-based lessons let learners focus on practical themes like food, travel, and family. On the practice side, Lingua Atlas turns learning into an active, game-like experience. Vocabulary quizzes, listening games, sentence builders, and flashcards reinforce what's been studied, while a built-in pronunciation feature uses speech recognition to score how accurately a learner speaks words aloud, giving instant feedback. A battle mode lets learners test their knowledge against friends or a computer opponent. Progress is tracked throughout, with daily goals, weekly challenges, streaks, and achievements. A built-in AI assistant, Scout, is also available for free-form chat, translation, and additional voice practice.
