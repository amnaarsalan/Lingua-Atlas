Lingua Atlas
Lingua Atlas is a mobile app for learning 21 languages, including Spanish, French, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese.

What it does
Study: vocabulary word banks, alphabet & numbers, grammar rules, proverbs, culture, and topic-based lessons
Practice: vocabulary quizzes, listening games, sentence builders, flashcards, and pronunciation scoring with speech recognition
Compete: battle mode against friends or bots, daily challenges, streaks, and a leaderboard
Scout AI: built-in AI chat, translation, and voice practice
Track progress: XP, daily goals, weekly challenges, and achievements
Available on iPhone, iPad, and Android.
